Our Heritage

A House with a
Hundred Years of Stories

Long before it welcomed guests, Laxmi Tharavadu was simply home — a traditional Kerala Tharavadu built in the 1920s, where a family of nine lived beneath these same rafters. The carved wooden beams, the central nadumuttam with its lotus pond, the oonjal swinging in the breeze — none of it was built for aesthetics. It was built for life.

"It's not about luxury, but about calmness and simplicity." A guest who found her peace here

Today, that life is yours to borrow. Set amidst a deliberately cultivated Miyawaki forest — a dense, biodiverse native woodland that draws birds and butterflies from across North Kerala — Laxmi is genuinely quiet in a way most places only promise. A Naga temple at the property's edge, organic paddy nearby, and a caretaker who knows every corner. This is Laxmi Tharavadu: unhurried, unhidden, entirely itself.

Life at Laxmi

The Things You'll
Actually Remember

01

The Miyawaki Forest

The grounds are planted using the Miyawaki method — dense, native woodland that grows fast and thick, drawing birds and wildlife unique to North Kerala. Walk through it in the morning mist and you understand why guests stop checking their phones.

02

The Nadumuttam

The central courtyard — soul of every Tharavadu. At Laxmi, a lotus pond sits at its heart. Sit by it in the afternoon. Photographers and content creators already know what this space does to a frame and to a person.

03

Bonfire Evenings

The garden comes alive after sundown. A bonfire, a BBQ if you like, and the kind of conversation that only happens when there's nothing else asking for your attention.

04

Photography Shoots

Pre-wedding shoots, reels, brand campaigns — Laxmi has become one of Kannur's most-sought backdrops. The oonjal, carved beams, lotus pond. It photographs like it was built for it. In a way, it was.

05

Morning Bird Chorus

Guests consistently mention the birds — not as an afterthought but as a highlight. The Miyawaki forest draws species most of Kerala doesn't hear anymore. Your mornings here will sound different.

06

Kerala on the Plate

Breakfast is included. Beyond that, the shared kitchen is yours. Our caretaker can source fresh local groceries and arrange Kerala-style meals — the way a home, not a hotel, feeds you.

Frequently Asked

Before You Pack Your Bags

How many rooms are there?▼

We have 4 rooms — The Patio Room, The Nadumuttam Room, and The Attic Room in the traditional heritage block, and The Garden Room in a separate garden wing. Each has a queen bed and a private attached bathroom.

Is breakfast included?▼

Yes, complimentary breakfast is included with every booking. We serve Kerala-style or English breakfast. Lunch and dinner can be arranged at approximately ₹180 per person — just let us know when you book.

Is the property air-conditioned?▼

The rooms are not air-conditioned. The dense Miyawaki forest surrounding the property provides natural cooling most guests find very comfortable. Each room has a ceiling fan and standing fan.

Are pets allowed?▼

Yes — Laxmi Tharavadu is proudly pet-friendly. Furry companions are very welcome. The garden and open grounds give them plenty of space to explore.

What are check-in and check-out times?▼

Check-in is at 12:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. If you need early check-in or late check-out, reach us on WhatsApp and we will do our best to accommodate.

Can I book the whole property?▼

Yes. The entire home can be booked privately for up to 14 guests — ideal for family stays, friend trips, and celebrations. Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 97446 03000 for group rates.

Is WiFi available?▼

Yes, complimentary WiFi is available throughout the property. Though honestly, once you are here, most guests find themselves using it rather less than usual.

How far is the nearest airport and railway?▼

Kannur International Airport (CNN) is approximately 40 km away. Kannapuram Railway Station is 8.5 km away. We can arrange pickup and drop — just ask when booking.

Is it suitable for photography shoots?▼

It is one of Kannur's most sought-after locations for photography. The lotus pond courtyard, the oonjal, carved heritage beams, and the lush garden create frames very hard to manufacture elsewhere. Contact us in advance to plan your dates.

What is a Tharavadu?▼

A Tharavadu is a traditional Kerala ancestral home, passed down through generations. Laxmi Tharavadu was built in the 1920s and once housed a family of nine. The original architecture — central nadumuttam with lotus pond, carved wooden pillars, and the oonjal — is preserved.

Is parking available?▼

Yes, free onsite parking is available for up to 3 cars.

What is the cancellation policy?▼

Free cancellation is available until 11:59 AM the day before check-in for direct bookings. For Airbnb and MakeMyTrip bookings, their respective platform policies apply.
