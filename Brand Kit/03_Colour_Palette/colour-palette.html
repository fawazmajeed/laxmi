Colour Usage Principles
On Dark Backgrounds
Use Cream or Ivory for primary text. Use Brand Gold for headings and accents. Use Sage Green for secondary text. Never use Earth Brown on Forest Green.
On Light Backgrounds
Use Forest Green or Gold Dark for primary text. Use Fern Green for accents and labels. Brand Gold (#F0B800) should only appear as decorative accent, not as large text on white.
