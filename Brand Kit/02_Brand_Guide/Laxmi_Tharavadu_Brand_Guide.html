01  ·  Brand Story
Who we are.
Laxmi Tharavadu is not a hotel. It is a home that happens to welcome guests. Nestled in the quiet of Koovode village in Thaliparamba, Kannur, it is a living heritage property where the forest presses against old walls, where monsoon mornings smell of wet earth, and where every room carries the weight of a family's history.

"Where the forest breathes and old walls hold a hundred stories."

The brand exists to offer travellers something that cannot be manufactured: genuine heritage, unhurried time, and a connection to a Kerala that is disappearing. Our guests are not tourists seeking amenities. They are seekers drawn to authenticity, nature, and the kind of stillness that can only be found in a place that has been loved for generations.
Authentic
Nothing is staged. The home, the garden, the food, the stories — all of it is real and lived-in.
Serene
Stillness is the product. Every design decision protects the atmosphere of unhurried calm.
Heritage
The property is a piece of Kerala's cultural memory. The brand honours that with dignity.
Premium
Not luxury in the hotel sense. Premium in honesty: everything is thoughtfully done and genuinely good.
Rooted
Deeply tied to Kannur, to Malayalam culture, to the land itself. This is not a pan-India brand.
Nature-immersed
The forest is the experience. The brand breathes green, lives in the monsoon, and speaks of the earth.
02  ·  Logo
The mark.
The Laxmi Tharavadu logo has two components: a circular icon featuring a stylised Malayalam calligraphy motif, and the wordmark "LAXMI" with "THARAVADU" set in Baloo 2 ExtraBold. The two elements must always appear together in the full logo. The icon alone may be used in circular avatar contexts (social media profile, stamp, key tag).

Do
Use the logo on approved brand background colours only
Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "L" on all sides
Scale proportionally — never stretch or compress
Use the icon alone only in circular/stamp contexts
Use the gold version as the primary digital version
Minimum size: 120px wide for digital, 30mm for print
Do Not
Do not recolour the logo to unapproved colours
Do not place on busy photographic backgrounds without overlay
Do not add shadows, glows, or effects
Do not rearrange the icon and wordmark
Do not use the grey logo on dark backgrounds
Do not enclose in a box unless it is an approved brand shape

04  ·  Typography
Baloo 2.
Baloo 2 is the sole brand typeface. A warm, rounded display font by Ek Type, it was originally designed with Indian scripts in mind. It pairs natural warmth with premium readability — perfectly aligned with the heritage and approachability of Laxmi Tharavadu.
LAXMI
Tharavadu Heritage Home

ExtraBold 800
Display / Headline
Logo wordmark, hero headlines, primary display text. The brand's voice at its fullest.
Bold 700
Subheadings / H2–H3
Section headings, card titles, navigation items. Strong but not dominant.
SemiBold 600
Labels / Overlines
Category labels, form fields, captions. Always uppercase with 2-3px tracking when used at small sizes.
05  ·  Photography Style
The visual world.
Photography for Laxmi Tharavadu captures the sensory truth of the place — not the Instagram-styled version, but the real one. Lush, slightly moody, atmospheric, and always honest. Every image should make the viewer smell the rain on the earth.
Forest Light
Forest & Nature
Lush canopy, dappled light, rain on leaves. Deep greens, low contrast, soft shadows. Never over-saturated.
Heritage Interior
Heritage & Architecture
Clay tiles, carved wood, aged walls, Kerala-style arches. Warm amber light. No wide-angle distortion.
Monsoon Mood
Monsoon & Atmosphere
Rain, mist, grey light on green. Quiet, contemplative. The beauty of being indoors when it pours.
Morning Gold
Morning Light
Sunrise gold through trees, dew on garden, the first cup of tea. Warm, slow, unhurried.
Kerala Food
Food & Hospitality
Traditional Kerala meals on banana leaf, earthenware, copper vessels. Natural top-down or 45 degree angle. No studio lighting.
Lifestyle
Guest & Lifestyle
Guests reading, walking the garden, sitting by the window. Candid, not posed. Human warmth without the performance.
Post-Processing Direction
ToneSlightly lifted shadows. Warm mid-tones. No cold blue casts. The mood is Kerala, not Scandinavia.
SaturationReduced, not boosted. Greens should feel real, not neon. Gold tones should glow, not scream.
Crop & CompositionGenerous negative space. Let the forest breathe in frame. Square for social, 3:2 for editorial.
06  ·  Brand Voice
How we speak.
Laxmi Tharavadu speaks the way a knowledgeable, warm host does — with quiet confidence, never loudly. The tone is literary but never academic. Emotional but never sentimental. Proud of heritage but never nostalgic in a cliched way.
Intimate
We speak to one person at a time, never to a crowd. "You" not "guests". "Our home" not "the property".
Say this
"Come and stay. We have a room that faces the forest."
Not this
"Guests are welcomed to enjoy our various room options."
Honest
No marketing fluff. No "luxury experience" if it is not a hotel. What it is, said clearly and with pride.
Say this
"This is a home. Lived in. Real. That is the point."
Not this
"Experience unparalleled luxury at our premium heritage retreat."
Sensory
Ground every description in something you can smell, hear, feel, or taste. Abstract beauties need not apply.
Say this
"Breakfast is on the verandah. You will hear the forest before you see it."
Not this
"Enjoy a wonderful and memorable dining experience."
Unhurried
Short sentences. No rush. Let words breathe. The pace of the copy should feel like the pace of the place.
Say this
"No rush. The forest is patient. So are we."
Not this
"Book now and take advantage of our early-bird offers and package deals!"
