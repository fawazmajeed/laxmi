Usage Rules

Display / Hero Headlines

Weight 800 ExtraBold. Use for the brand name, hero text, and large format headlines. May be set in Brand Gold on dark backgrounds or Forest Green on light.

Section Headings (H2, H3)

Weight 700 Bold. Subheadings, section titles, card headlines. Use Forest Green on light backgrounds. Use Cream on dark backgrounds.

Labels & Overlines

Weight 600 SemiBold, uppercase, tracked at 2-3px letter-spacing. Used for category labels, section markers, form field labels, and UI elements.

Body Text

Weight 400 Regular at 15-16px, line-height 1.7-1.8. Use for all descriptive content, paragraph text, and captions. Color: #555 on light, Sage Green on dark.

Scale Reference

H1: 48-64px  ·  H2: 32-40px  ·  H3: 20-28px  ·  H4/Label: 11-14px uppercase  ·  Body: 14-16px  ·  Caption: 12px

Do Not Mix

Baloo 2 is the sole brand typeface. Do not pair it with other display fonts. For long-form editorial content (brochures, long emails), a neutral system serif may supplement body paragraphs only.
